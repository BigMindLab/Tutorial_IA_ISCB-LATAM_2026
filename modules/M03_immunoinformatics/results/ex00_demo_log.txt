[question 1] Does fhaC enter the secretory pathway? (slide 18)
  DeepTMHMM also predicts a signal peptide, ending at 32.
  UniProt annotates it ending at 30.
  difference: +2 residues
  Two independent predictions of the same boundary is already
  something to compare, before SignalP is even installed.

  SignalP output not supplied, so only the reference is shown.
  UniProt annotates a signal peptide at 1-30.
  Run ex7_signalp6.py once SignalP 6.0 is installed, then pass
  --signalp results/signalp6/prediction_results.txt to compare.

  Predict before you run it: fhaC is from a Gram negative bacterium,
  so the organism argument is 'other', not 'eukarya'. Which of the five
  signal peptide types on slide 18 do you expect, and why?

[question 2] Is it a membrane protein, and which way round? (slide 19)
  UniProt annotates 16 transmembrane beta strands.
  >fhaC | BETA  length=584
  label counts: {'S': 32, 'P': 272, 'B': 138, 'O': 142}
  predicted beta strands: 16
  UniProt beta strands  : 16
  raw per residue agreement: 47.3%
  That number looks poor, and it is misleading. The reference
  marks every non strand residue as 'O' because UniProt does not
  label inside and outside, so every 'P' DeepTMHMM predicts is
  counted as a mismatch even when it is right.
  agreement on the strand or not strand question: 88.4%
  That is the comparison the reference can actually support.

[why this matters downstream]
  The first 30 residues are cut off during secretion, so they are not
  part of the mature protein. Any class I or class II candidate that
  falls entirely inside 1-30 should be dropped before ranking.
    9-mers fully inside the signal peptide: 22
    15-mers fully inside the signal peptide: 16

  That is the first filter of the prioritisation exercise, and it comes
  from slide 18, not from any binding predictor.

QUESTIONS TO DISCUSS
==========================================================================
  1. SignalP gives a signal peptide type and a cleavage site. Neither
     says anything about antigenicity. What does each one actually
     constrain in the rest of this workflow?
  2. The reference here is curated annotation, much of it from a solved
     structure. If a predictor disagrees with it, is the predictor wrong?
  3. DeepTMHMM labels every residue. SignalP labels only the N terminus.
     Why does that difference matter when you chain them together?
  4. fhaC is an extracellular bacterial protein. Which MHC class is the
     natural route for it, and what does that imply for exercises that
     only predict class I? (Look back at slide 20.)

[14.5] And TIDE? The other half of the slide
      TIDE appears nowhere in any of the above, and that is the point.
      Its input is the expression profile of a TUMOUR and its output is
      ONE score per tumour, not per peptide. The slide says it outright:
      'No peptide, no HLA allele, no epitope anywhere in this diagram'.

      The two questions side by side:
            aspect           pVACtools (what we did)                             TIDE
  unit of analysis                 peptide by allele           one tumour, one sample
             input protein plus HLA plus predictions RNA-seq or NanoString expression
            output               ranked epitope list      a checkpoint response score
        needs HLA?                               yes                               no
needs the antigen?                               yes                               no
   works for fhaC?                               yes   no, fhaC is a pathogen antigen

      results/ex14_pvactools_vs_tide.csv
      Closing line from the slide: 'Pathogen epitopes and tumour
      neoantigens are different problems'. A neoantigen needs the
      patient's own HLA type and sequencing of THEIR tumour. fhaC does not.

  1. We chose the weights of the combined score ourselves. Change them
     and watch the top 15 move. What does that say about a shortlist?
  2. We filtered on the median immunogenicity. Why a median rather than
     an absolute threshold? What is wrong with each option?
  3. The TCR_evidence column is empty. What experiment would fill it?
  4. We did not use conservation across B. pertussis strains. Why would
     that be the obvious next filter for a vaccine?
  5. fhaC reaches the immune system as an extracellular protein, so its
     natural route is MHC class II. What does that make this class I
     shortlist, and which exercise would you run next?
