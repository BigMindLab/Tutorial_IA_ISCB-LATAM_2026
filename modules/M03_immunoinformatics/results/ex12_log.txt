[12.4] [TRAINING] THE POINT: presentation is not immunogenicity
      AUC from the presentation score alone : 0.582
      AUC from the immunogenicity CNN       : 0.853
      correlation between the two scores    : r=0.13

      Read that carefully. The presentation score does separate the
      classes a little, it is above 0.5, but far less than a model
      trained on the right question. And the two scores barely
      correlate, meaning they order the peptides by different
      criteria. That is the slide phrase, 'a strong binder is not
      necessarily immunogenic', turned into two numbers.

      Beware the easy shortcut. Of the 20 best presented peptides in
      the test set, 18/20 are immunogenic in IEDB.
      That looks like presentation is enough, but it is an artefact of
      SELECTION BIAS. Peptides reach IEDB because somebody already
      suspected they would bind, not by sampling the proteome at random.
      The overall AUC of the presentation score stays low (0.582) even
      though the head of the ranking looks clean.
      Methodological moral: look at the whole curve, not the top k.
      figure written to results/ex12_4_presentation_vs_immunogenicity.png

==============================================================================
QUESTIONS TO DISCUSS
==============================================================================
  1. The AUC drops when the split is grouped by peptide. Why would an
     ordinary random split give a prettier and more dishonest number?
  2. PRIME leaves P2 and the last position out of the frequency vector.
     What biological assumption is behind that? Does it hold for class II?
  3. The slide says DeepImmuno reports low precision on SARS-CoV-2.
     Looking at the allele composition of the training set, why?
  4. The 9,055 peptides come from IEDB assays. Does 'Negative' mean
     'not immunogenic' or 'nobody detected a response in that assay'?
  5. fhaC is an extracellular bacterial protein. Its natural route is
     MHC class II. What does that make this whole class I exercise?
