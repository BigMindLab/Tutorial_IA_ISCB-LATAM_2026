==============================================================================
QUESTIONS TO DISCUSS
==============================================================================
  1. The API gives the eluted ligand output and not binding affinity.
     Slide 21 says BA is trained on measured affinities and EL on
     peptides eluted from cells. Which one would you rank on, and why?
  2. Compare these class I hits against MHCflurry and MixMHCpred. How
     many peptides do all three call a strong binder?
  3. Class II uses a threshold of 2 where class I uses 0.5. Why can a
     looser threshold still be the right call for class II?
  4. A class II 15-mer is scored through a 9-mer core. Look at the core
     column: do several 15-mers share one core? What does that mean for
     counting candidates?
