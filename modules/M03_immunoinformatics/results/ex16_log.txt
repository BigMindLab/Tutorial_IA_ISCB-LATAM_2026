QUESTIONS TO DISCUSS
==============================================================================
  1. Agreement between three tools is reassuring. Could they agree and
     all be wrong together? What would they have in common that would
     cause that?
  2. We ranked by the WORST rank across tools. What changes if you rank
     by the average instead, and which is the honest default?
  3. A dual class I and class II region looks ideal. What experiment
     would you run first to test it?
  4. MixMHCpred is not a neural network. Does that make its agreement
     with the other two worth more, or less?
